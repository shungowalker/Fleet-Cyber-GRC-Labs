# Week 01: Fleet Operations & Evidence Rigor

**Scenario:** Heartland Freight (fictional), a 500-truck carrier with three dispatch centers, is under cybersecurity due diligence for a major retail contract. My role: newly hired **Fleet Technology Security Coordinator**.

| Lab | Focus |
|---|---|
| [Lab 1: Asset inventory](lab-1-asset-inventory/) | Know what you have, who owns it, how sensitive it is, and where card data might be |
| [Lab 2: Evidence rejection](lab-2-evidence-rejection/) | Reject a supplier's "trust us" letter and request evidence that can actually be verified |

**Key lesson:** Compliance is never claimed from a single artifact or a supplier's assurance. It is shown with evidence that is scoped, dated, traceable and verifiable.
