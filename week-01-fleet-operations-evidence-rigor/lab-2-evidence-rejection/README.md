# Lab 2: TruckTrack GPS Evidence Rejection Notice

**Objective:** Assess a supplier's response to control **TISAX-04, Secure Configuration Policy** (a fictional training control ID), reject it if insufficient, and issue a firm, courteous, actionable request for replacement evidence with a deadline and escalation path.

📄 **Files:** [Word notice](TruckTrack_Evidence_Rejection_SMW.docx) · [PDF preview](TruckTrack_Evidence_Rejection_SMW.pdf)

## The submission
An unsigned, undated letter: *"We configure all our servers securely, follow industry best practices… Please accept this letter as proof of our compliance."*

## Assessment
| | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Design effectiveness | ❌ Not shown: no documented hardening baseline |
| Operating effectiveness | ❌ Not shown: no results, records or observation period |

**8 missing evidence attributes:** system scope/population · applicable configuration baseline · configuration results · date and observation period · provenance/authenticity · independent verification · exception and change management · accountability and approval

## Replacement evidence requested
1. Approved hardening standard (version, date, approver, benchmark basis)
2. Scope statement / system inventory, including in-truck devices and firmware
3. Dated configuration compliance results covering the full population
4. A 90-day observation period with remediation records
5. Exception register *(if available)*
6. Independent assurance, e.g. a TISAX label, ISO/IEC 27001 certificate or SOC 2 Type II report *(if available)*

## Deadline and escalation
Five business days → **Level 0** open with the supplier → **Level 1** recorded finding + CAPA via Vendor Management → **Level 2** executive sponsor; affects supplier risk rating.

**Key lesson:** Screenshots and exports aren't automatically weak, and letters aren't automatically strong. Evidence is judged on relevance, source, date, scope, completeness and whether it can be corroborated.
